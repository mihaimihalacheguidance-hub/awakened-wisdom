A free habit tracker that doesn't reset your progress

Most habit trackers wipe your streak to zero after one missed day, and that is often the day people quit. Awakened Wisdom uses one rule instead: never miss twice. One missed day is normal. Two in a row is where habits fade, so the app shows you when to restart, and the days you already did still count.

What is "never miss twice"?

Miss Wednesday? Thursday is the day that counts. Missed two in a row? Make the practice smaller, but don't stop. A gap stays a gap; it never erases your run.

A small accountability circle, free

Pick one small daily practice, set a cue that starts it (like "after my morning coffee"), and invite a small circle of up to 8 people with a code. They see that you showed up, and only what you choose to share.

Why no streak punishment

No ads, no paywall, no AI inside, and no sign-up needed to start. You can delete everything at any time from the You tab.
